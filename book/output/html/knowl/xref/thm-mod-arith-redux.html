Theorem 9.4.2. Modular arithmetic redux.

Let \(n \in \mathbb{N}\text{,}\) and let \(a,b,c,d \in \mathbb{Z}\) so that
\begin{equation*} c \in [a] \qquad \text{and} \qquad d \in [b], \end{equation*}
where \([x]\) denotes the equivalence class of \(x\) under congruence modulo \(n\text{.}\) Then
\begin{align*} c+d \amp \in [a+b], \amp c-d \amp \in [a-b] \amp \text{and}\\ c\cdot d \amp \in [a\cdot b] \end{align*}

🔗

Proof.
The proof of this statement is essentially identical to the proof of Theorem 5.3.3.
