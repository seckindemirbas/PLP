Lemma 12.6.2.

Let \((a_n)\) be a sequence of real numbers that
  • is bounded above by some real number \(M\text{,}\) and
    🔗
  • satisfies \(a_k \leq a_{k+1}\) for all \(k\text{.}\)
    🔗
Then the sequence \(a_n\) converges as \(n \to \infty\text{.}\)
